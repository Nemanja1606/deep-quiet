Deep Quiet Uploader

Deep Quiet Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Deep Quiet (calm sleep documentaries about the deep sea and outer space).

It uploads the channel's own finished videos with title, description, translations, thumbnail and subtitles through the YouTube Data API, schedules them, and reads the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. It is not offered to other users.
