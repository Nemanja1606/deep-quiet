Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Deep Quiet Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool run by Nemanja Vucetic on his own computer. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channel Deep Quiet.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • The channel owner's own YouTube channel: uploading and updating its videos, thumbnails and captions.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, likes, estimated revenue).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's videos and to show the owner his own statistics. The OAuth token and any statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data of other YouTube users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept locally for as long as the owner needs them and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; the local token is then deleted.
